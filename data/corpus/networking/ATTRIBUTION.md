## What was changed here

This is **not** a verbatim conversion of the textbook and is not an official edition. The material in this folder has been:

- reorganized around beginner networking concepts rather than textbook chapter order;
- rewritten in simpler language;
- condensed to focus on operational understanding;
- supplemented with original examples, ASCII diagrams, troubleshooting sequences, and UC/voice context;
- split into small Markdown documents suitable for human study or retrieval-augmented generation (RAG);
- cross-referenced to the relevant upstream source paths and standards.

No endorsement by the original authors is implied.
