# Troubleshoot Microsoft Teams Voice Issues

- Module
- 6 Units

 Advanced

We will look at how to monitor and troubleshoot audio and video quality issues, together with emergency calling and direct routing issues.

## Learning objectives

After completing this module, you’ll be able to:

- Understand and troubleshoot media flow.
- Use the Call Quality Dashboard to investigate audio and video issues.
- Troubleshoot emergency calling issues.
- Troubleshoot direct routing issues.

## Prerequisites

- Experience configuring and administering Microsoft Teams
- Experience using Windows PowerShell
- Working experience with performance tuning and service monitoring
